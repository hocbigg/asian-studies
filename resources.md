# Resources

## Primary Text & Archival Repositories

Digital collections providing open access to digitized manuscripts, imperial records, pre-modern corpora, and declassified diplomatic documents across Asia.

- [Chinese Text Project (Donald Sturgeon)](https://ctext.org/) - Open-access digital library and concordance engine of pre-modern Chinese texts spanning antiquity through the Qing dynasty, featuring character lookups and parallel English translations.
- [Korean History Database (National Institute of Korean History)](https://db.history.go.kr/) - Comprehensive public repository of digitized primary documents, royal annals, diplomatic records, and modern periodicals covering ancient through contemporary Korean history.
- [National Diet Library Digital Collections (National Diet Library, Japan)](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/en/) - Extensive searchable archive of digitized rare books, historical periodicals, public records, and out-of-print materials preserved by Japan's national library.
- [South Asia Open Archives (Center for Research Libraries & JSTOR)](https://www.jstor.org/site/saoa/) - Freely accessible repository containing over one million pages of historical South Asian periodicals, colonial administrative records, and cultural publications across dozens of regional languages.
- [Southeast Asia Digital Library (Northern Illinois University & CORMOSEA)](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/) - Curated digital clearinghouse aggregating rare manuscripts, historical photographs, oral histories, and cultural records from mainland and maritime Southeast Asia.
- [International Dunhuang Programme (British Library)](https://idp.bl.uk/) - Global collaborative database offering high-resolution imagery and scholarly cataloging of over 100,000 manuscripts, paintings, and textiles recovered from Dunhuang and Eastern Silk Road sites.
- [Wilson Center Digital Archive: International History Declassified (Woodrow Wilson International Center for Scholars)](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/) - Searchable archive of translated, declassified diplomatic cables and intelligence reports detailing Cold War Asia, the Korean War, Sino-Soviet relations, and Chinese foreign policy.

## Historical GIS & Cartographic Databases

Spatial research engines, historical boundary datasets, and map archives used to trace territorial changes, trade corridors, and regional geography.

- [China Historical GIS (Harvard University & Fudan University)](https://chgis.fas.harvard.edu/) - Authoritative spatial database tracking changes in administrative units, county seats, and jurisdictional borders across Chinese history from 221 BCE to 1911 CE.
- [Perry-Castañeda Library Map Collection: Asia (University of Texas at Austin)](https://maps.lib.utexas.edu/maps/asia.html) - Extensive digital repository of public-domain historical, topographic, and geopolitical maps of Asian nations and subregions from the 19th century onward.
- [Digital Silk Road Project (National Institute of Informatics, Japan)](http://dsr.nii.ac.jp/) - Digital humanities platform providing georeferenced historical maps, archaeological ruin coordinates, and digitized expedition travelogues along Eurasian trade routes.

## Biographical, Lexicographical & Research Tools

Scholarly reference lexicons, classical terminology databases, and prosopographical directories for researching Asian historical actors and canonical terms.

- [China Biographical Database (Harvard University, Academia Sinica, & Peking University)](https://cbdb.fas.harvard.edu/) - Searchable relational database recording biographical details, kinship networks, and bureaucratic postings for over 500,000 historical Chinese figures.
- [Digital Dictionaries of South Asia (University of Chicago)](https://dsal.uchicago.edu/dictionaries/) - Searchable digital portal offering open access to classic monolingual and bilingual dictionaries across more than twenty South Asian languages.
- [Digital Dictionary of Buddhism (Charles Muller / University of Tokyo)](http://www.buddhism-dict.net/ddb/) - Scholarly multilingual lexicon covering Buddhist doctrinal terminology, canonical titles, and monastic figures across Sanskrit, Chinese, Tibetan, and Japanese, accessible via free guest login.

## Art, Archaeology & Material Culture Collections

Open museum collections, archaeological catalogs, and high-resolution galleries documenting visual culture and sacred material traditions across Asian civilizations.

- [National Museum of Asian Art Collections (Smithsonian Institution)](https://asia.si.edu/) - Searchable open-access catalog containing high-resolution photographs and curatorial entries for thousands of South, Southeast, and East Asian artworks and archaeological finds.
- [e-Museum: National Treasures & Important Cultural Properties (National Institutes for Cultural Heritage, Japan)](https://emuseum.nich.go.jp/) - Multilingual high-definition database showcasing designated Japanese national treasures, ancient manuscripts, screens, and Buddhist sculpture across Japan's four national museums.

## Contemporary Policy, Field Analysis & Scholarly Networks

Open-access platforms, research blogs, and academic networks that provide peer-reviewed commentary and professional discussion on Asian humanities and current affairs.

- [East Asia Forum (Crawford School of Public Policy, Australian National University)](https://eastasiaforum.org/) - Open-access platform publishing daily research articles and quarterly thematic issues on economics, politics, and governance across the Asia-Pacific.
- [New Mandala (Coral Bell School of Asia Pacific Affairs, Australian National University)](https://www.newmandala.org/) - Academic field-analysis site offering critical commentary, political reporting, and social analysis focused specifically on Southeast Asian societies.
- [H-Asia (H-Net Discussion Network)](https://networks.h-net.org/h-asia) - Moderated scholarly network featuring academic book reviews, conference announcements, research inquiries, and debates across Asian history and humanities.

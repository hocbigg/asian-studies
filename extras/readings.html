Great Readings in Asian Studies

This curated bibliography brings together transformative monographs, seminal essays, paradigm-shifting empirical studies, and foundational cultural texts in Asian Studies. Rather than introductory surveys, these works showcase how scholars, philosophers, and writers have formulated theoretical frameworks, unsettled Eurocentric assumptions, and analyzed the social, political, and ecological landscapes of Asia.

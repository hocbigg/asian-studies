# Great Courses in Asian Studies

This curated collection gathers exceptional university lecture series, full OpenCourseWare (OCW) syllabi, and high-production MOOCs in Asian Studies. These courses go beyond basic introductory surveys to offer immersive explorations of classical civilizations, sacred texts, diplomatic history, developmental political economy, visual culture, and environmental humanities.

## Civilizational Foundations and Classical Eras

- [ChinaX: China's Past, Present and Future (Harvard University / edX / Peter K. Bol & William C. Kirby)](https://www.edx.org/school/harvardx)
- [Cosmopolitan Tang: Aristocratic Culture in China (Harvard University / edX / Peter K. Bol)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/cosmopolitan-tang-aristocratic-culture-china)
- [Early Civilizations of South Asia (NPTEL / Swayam / IIT Madras)](https://nptel.ac.in/)
- [Visualizing Japan (1850s–1930s): Westernization, Protest, Modernity (MITx & HarvardX / edX / John W. Dower, Andrew Gordon, Shigeru Miyagawa)](https://www.edx.org/xseries/visualizing-japan)
- [Asia for Educators: Historical Foundations and Primary Sources (Columbia University)](https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/)

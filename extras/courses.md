# Great Courses in Asian Studies

This curated collection gathers exceptional university lecture series, OpenCourseWare (OCW) video courses, and high-production MOOCs in Asian Studies. These audiovisual courses move beyond basic surveys to offer immersive explorations of classical civilizations, sacred texts, diplomatic history, developmental political economy, visual culture, and environmental humanities.

## Classical Civilizations, Dynasties, and Visual Historiography

- [ChinaX: China's Past, Present and Future (Harvard University / edX / Peter K. Bol & William C. Kirby)](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/chinax)
- [Cosmopolitan Tang: Aristocratic Culture in China (Harvard University / edX / Peter K. Bol)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/cosmopolitan-tang-aristocratic-culture-china)
- [Classics of Chinese Humanities: Guided Readings (The Chinese University of Hong Kong / Coursera / Leo Ou-fan Lee)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/classics-chinese-humanities)
- [Visualizing Japan (1850s–1930s): Westernization, Protest, Modernity (MITx & HarvardX / edX / John W. Dower, Andrew Gordon, & Shigeru Miyagawa)](https://mitxonline.mit.edu/courses/course-v1:MITx+VJx/)
- [An Interpretive History of Japan (Stanford University / SPICE / Daniel Okimoto)](https://spice.fsi.stanford.edu/multimedia/interpretive-history-japan)

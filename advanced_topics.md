# Advanced Topics in Asian Studies

After completing the core undergraduate foundation in Pan-Asian history, classical philosophies, regional literatures, and modern institutions, advanced learners branch out into specialized research trajectories. This advanced curriculum provides five interdisciplinary specialization tracks designed for upper-division research, independent inquiry, and applied policy analysis.

- [Geopolitics, Security, and Strategic Competition in the Indo-Pacific](#geopolitics-security-and-strategic-competition-in-the-indo-pacific): Investigates great-power competition, maritime flashpoints, nuclear deterrence doctrines, and regional security architectures across the Indo-Pacific corridor.
- [Comparative Political Economy and State Transformations](#comparative-political-economy-and-state-transformations): Analyzes developmental state models, industrial policies, state capitalism, agrarian shifts, and infrastructure statecraft across Asia.
- [Critical Asian Historiography, Decolonial Methodologies, and Subalternity](#critical-asian-historiography-decolonial-methodologies-and-subalternity): Explores Subaltern Studies, borderland spatialities, "Asia as Method," and critical approaches to knowledge production.
- [Religion, Secularism, and Identity Politics in Modern Asia](#religion-secularism-and-identity-politics-in-modern-asia): Examines modern religious nationalisms, state-religion institutional boundaries, caste assertion, and politicized spiritual movements.
- [Environmental Humanities, Hydropolitics, and Climate Governance](#environmental-humanities-hydropolitics-and-climate-governance): Interrogates transboundary river basins, monsoon ecologies, critical mineral extraction, and atmospheric governance across Asia.

## Geopolitics, Security, and Strategic Competition in the Indo-Pacific

Investigates great-power competition, maritime flashpoints, nuclear deterrence doctrines, and regional security architectures across the Indo-Pacific corridor.

Hegemonic Rivalry and US-China Strategic Competition: [By More Than Providence: Grand Strategy and American Power in the Asia Pacific Since 1783 (Michael J. Green/Columbia University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780231180429) - Traces the historical evolution of US grand strategy and balance-of-power diplomacy across East Asia since the founding era.

Hegemonic Rivalry and US-China Strategic Competition: [Chinese Foreign Policy: International Relations and Strategy (MIT OpenCourseWare/M. Taylor Fravel)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-407-chinese-foreign-policy-international-relations-and-strategy-spring-2009/) - Upper-level university syllabus and analytical project frameworks examining Chinese deterrence, territorial disputes, and grand strategy.

Maritime Security and Contested Sovereignty in the South China Sea: [The South China Sea: The Struggle for Power in Asia (Bill Hayton/Yale University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780300216943) - Investigates the historical claims, international law of the sea (UNCLOS), resource competition, and naval rivalries in the South China Sea.

Regional Security Architectures and ASEAN Centrality: [The Making of Southeast Asia: International Relations of a Region (Amitav Acharya/Cornell University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780801477362) - Provides a foundational constructivist analysis of regional identity, norm localization, and the institutional dynamics of ASEAN diplomacy.

Nuclear Deterrence and Strategic Postures in Asia: [Nuclear Strategy in the Modern Era: Regional Powers and International Conflict (Vipin Narang/Princeton University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691159836) - Unpacks the distinct nuclear postures—catalytic, assured retaliation, and asymmetric escalation—governing strategic stability across China, India, and Pakistan.

Cross-Strait Geopolitics and Taiwan's Security: [Why Taiwan Matters: Small Island, Global Powerhouse (Shelley Rigger/Rowman & Littlefield)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781442204799) - Analyzes Taiwan's democratic development, global semiconductor centrality, and asymmetric deterrence options amid rising cross-strait tensions.

## Comparative Political Economy and State Transformations

Analyzes developmental state models, industrial policies, state capitalism, agrarian shifts, and infrastructure statecraft across Asia.

The East Asian Developmental State Model: [Governing the Market: Economic Theory and the Role of Government in East Asian Industrialization (Robert Wade/Princeton University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691117294) - Seminal theoretical text explaining how state-guided investment, bureaucratic discipline, and industrial targeting shaped East Asian late industrialization.

State Capitalism and China's Political Economy: [China's Gilded Age: The Paradox of Economic Boom and Vast Corruption (Yuen Yuen Ang/Cambridge University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781108478601) - Examines the political economy of access money, local state corporatism, and regulatory risk under China's market-Leninist growth model.

Agrarian Transitions and Critical Land Politics: [Powers of Exclusion: Land Dilemmas in Southeast Asia (Derek Hall, Philip Hirsch, & Tania Murray Li/NUS Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9789971695415) - Investigates how state regulation, market forces, force, and legitimation dispossess smallholder farmers and transform agrarian landscapes across Southeast Asia.

Infrastructure Statecraft and Geo-Economic Connectivity: [Rivers of Iron: Railroads and Chinese Power in Southeast Asia (David M. Lampton, Selina Ho, & Cheng-Chwee Kuik/University of California Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780520372993) - Details how mega-railway infrastructure projects are negotiated, contested, and leveraged by Southeast Asian nations balancing Chinese influence.

Authoritarian Resilience and Regime Durability: [Ordering Power: Contentious Politics and Authoritarian Leviathans in Southeast Asia (Dan Slater/Cambridge University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780521165457) - Develops a historical-institutionalist theory showing how elite protection pacts formed against mass unrest fostered durable authoritarian regimes in Southeast Asia.

## Critical Asian Historiography, Decolonial Methodologies, and Subalternity

Explores Subaltern Studies, borderland spatialities, "Asia as Method," and critical approaches to knowledge production.

Subaltern Studies and Postcolonial Historiographical Critique: [Provincializing Europe: Postcolonial Thought and Historical Difference (Dipesh Chakrabarty/Princeton University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691130019) - Landmark theoretical critique demonstrating how modern historical scholarship universalizes European conceptual categories and how non-Western modernities must be rethought.

"Asia as Method" and Decolonial Cultural Studies: [Asia as Method: Toward Deimperialization (Kuan-Hsing Chen/Duke University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780822346760) - Proposes using Asian societies as mutual reference points for theory-building rather than relying solely on Western intellectual frameworks.

Zomia and Non-State Frontier Spatialities: [The Art of Not Being Governed: An Anarchist History of Upland Southeast Asia (James C. Scott/Yale University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780300156522) - Theorizes upland Asia as an intentional refuge from lowland state-making, taxation, and conscription.

Afro-Asian Solidarity, Bandung, and Non-Aligned Internationalism: [Bandung, Global History, and International Law: Critical Pasts and Pending Futures (Luis Eslava, Michael Fakhri, & Vasuki Nesiah, eds./Cambridge University Press)](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781316414880) - Open-access volume reinterpreting the 1955 Bandung Conference, postcolonial sovereignty, and Third World legal and political projects.

## Religion, Secularism, and Identity Politics in Modern Asia

Examines modern religious nationalisms, state-religion institutional boundaries, caste assertion, and politicized spiritual movements.

Majoritarianism and Hindu Nationalism in South Asia: [Majoritarian State: How Hindu Nationalism Is Changing India (Angana P. Chatterji, Thomas Blom Hansen, & Christophe Jaffrelot, eds./Oxford University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780190078171) - Comprehensive volume analyzing the ideological shifts, legal restructuring, and statecraft underpinning the rise of Hindu majoritarianism.

Theravada Monastic Nationalism and State Violence: [Buddhist Warfare (Michael Jerryson & Mark Juergensmeyer, eds./Oxford University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195394849) - Interrogates the relationship between Buddhist doctrine, monastic political mobilization, and state-sanctioned violence across South and Southeast Asia.

State Regulation of Religion in Modern China: [The Religious Question in Modern China (Vincent Goossaert & David A. Palmer/University of Chicago Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780226304168) - Traces how Chinese regimes from the late Qing to the contemporary Communist state have categorized, suppressed, and co-opted religious traditions.

Political Islam and Democratization in Southeast Asia: [Civil Islam: Muslims and Democratization in Indonesia (Robert W. Hefner/Princeton University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780691050478) - Demonstrates how Islamic civic organizations, intellectuals, and piety movements shaped public democracy and pluralism in modern Indonesia.

Caste Annihilation and Dalit Radical Thought: [Annihilation of Caste: The Annotated Critical Edition (B. R. Ambedkar/Verso)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781781688311) - Canonical philosophical text analyzing the structural violence of the caste hierarchy and outlining the intellectual foundations of Dalit liberation.

## Environmental Humanities, Hydropolitics, and Climate Governance

Interrogates transboundary river basins, monsoon ecologies, critical mineral extraction, and atmospheric governance across Asia.

Transboundary Hydropolitics in Asian River Basins: [Unruly Waters: How Mountain Rivers and Monsoons Have Shaped South Asia's History (Sunil Amrith/Basic Books)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780465097722) - Examines the interconnected history of monsoon water systems, state engineering projects, and transboundary water contestation across South and Southeast Asia.

Monsoon Ecologies and Planetary Climate Crisis: [The Great Derangement: Climate Change and the Unthinkable (Amitav Ghosh/University of Chicago Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780226323176) - Explores the historical, cultural, and political failures to confront accelerating ecological vulnerability across Asian deltaic coastlines.

Extractive Frontiers and Resource Colonization: [Nature, Culture, Imperialism: Essays on the Environmental History of South Asia (David Arnold & Ramachandra Guha, eds./Oxford University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195640755) - Landmark collection investigating colonial ecological disruption, forest enclosure, and peasant resource struggles in South Asia.

Megacity Urbanism and Critical Urban Ecologies: [Worlding Cities: Asian Experiments and the Art of Being Global (Ananya Roy & Aihwa Ong, eds./Wiley-Blackwell)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781405192767) - Investigates speculative urbanism, transboundary ecological footprints, and social inequality across emerging Asian mega-regions.
